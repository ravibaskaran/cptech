# Research: Features And BRD Scope

## Table Stakes For The BRD

### Business Context

- Justo's strategic goal: become a large regional CP in Maharashtra, then expand across India. [high]
- Project Manthan context: existing CRM foundation, delivered phases, and CP Tech adjacency. [high]
- Current vendor landscape: Auum, TSPL/Triazine, and I9/Indexnine proposals. [high]

### Personas

- Justo leadership. [high]
- Justo CP sourcing head. [high]
- Justo RM/sourcing employee. [high]
- Justo sales/admin ops. [high]
- Justo finance. [high]
- Developer/project team. [high]
- CP owner/org leader. [high]
- CP employee/agent. [high]
- CP telecaller. [moderate]
- Buyer/customer. [high]
- Compliance/support. [moderate]

### Lifecycles

- Justo CP sourcing lifecycle: prospecting, capture, verification, commercial setup, RM/project mapping, enablement, activation, performance management, retention/suspension/offboarding. [high]
- CP firm lifecycle: registration, documents, approval, agreement, profile, employees, project access, lead submission, site visit, booking, payout, support, renewal/offboarding. [high]
- CP employee lifecycle: invite/import, verification, role assignment, project/lead access, training, activity, performance, reassignment, deactivation. [high]
- Lead lifecycle: capture, dedupe, lock, assignment, communication, site visit, booking, payout linkage, dispute handling. [high]
- Payout lifecycle: commission rule, booking validation, invoice, GST/TDS, finance approval, payment, reconciliation, dispute. [high]

### Pain Points

- Stale inventory and untrusted project/pricing information. [moderate]
- Lead ownership conflict and duplicate disputes. [high]
- Delayed/opaque commission payouts. [high]
- Fragmented communication and follow-up leakage. [high]
- RERA/KYC/GST/bank compliance burden. [high]
- Weak CP employee controls. [high]
- Poor site-visit proof. [high]
- Low marketing/source ROI visibility. [moderate]
- Buyer trust gaps around claims, fees, and project information. [moderate]

### Vendor Comparison

- Capability matrix across Auum, TSPL, I9. [high]
- Manthan integration certainty. [high]
- CP sourcing/RM lifecycle coverage. [high]
- CP employee lifecycle coverage. [moderate]
- Lead ownership, site-visit proof, commission/payout, compliance, AI, delivery risk, commercial/IP/support. [high]

## Differentiators To Include As Recommendations

- CP trust score. [moderate]
- Payout promise and SLA visibility. [moderate]
- Inventory freshness badge. [moderate]
- CP Academy and project certification. [moderate]
- Claim-safe collateral governance. [moderate]
- RM field productivity and CP recruitment funnel. [moderate]
- Dispute-grade evidence trail. [moderate]

## Anti-Features For BRD V1

- Full product UI design. [high]
- Detailed system architecture rewrite. [high]
- Final vendor award recommendation. [high]
- Implementation sprint plan for app development. [high]
- Detailed AI voice call design unless it is framed as a deferred capability. [high]

## Dependencies

- Local source documents under `docs/`. [high]
- Existing `Justo_CP_App_Business_Brief.md`. [high]
- Stakeholder review to validate assumptions and priorities. [high]
- Optional CP/RM interviews to strengthen field evidence. [moderate]
