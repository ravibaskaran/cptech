# Research Summary

## Key Findings

**Stack:** The project is a BRD/documentation project, so Markdown plus traceable evidence tables are the right working format. The target ecosystem to reflect is Project Manthan plus a CP mobile/app operating layer, not a standalone greenfield app.

**Table Stakes:** The BRD must cover business objectives, Manthan baseline, personas, app/outside-app responsibilities, Justo CP sourcing, CP firm lifecycle, CP employee lifecycle, lead/site visit/booking/payout lifecycles, CP pain points, capability requirements, vendor gap mapping, MVP recommendations, and open decisions.

**Watch Out For:** The biggest risks are writing a generic CP app document, over-indexing on AI, failing to distinguish Manthan baseline from new CP operating capabilities, underbuilding CP employee lifecycle, and turning vendor gap analysis into unsupported procurement claims.

## BRD Implications

- The BRD should be organized around CP network lifecycle and trust loops.
- Requirements should be testable as document content requirements, not software implementation requirements yet.
- Vendor comparison should use "specified / partially specified / not specified" style evidence.
- The first milestone should produce a reviewable BRD draft before any app implementation planning.

## Recommended First BRD Draft Sections

1. Executive summary.
2. Business objectives.
3. Evidence base, assumptions, and confidence.
4. Manthan baseline.
5. Personas and responsibilities.
6. Lifecycle deep dives.
7. CP pain points and technology opportunities.
8. Capability requirements and MVP recommendation.
9. Vendor proposal mapping and gaps.
10. Risks, dependencies, open questions, and next decisions.
